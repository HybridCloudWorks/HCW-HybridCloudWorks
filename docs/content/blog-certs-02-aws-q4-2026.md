All thirteen AWS certification pages were re-read on October 5, 2026, with the
AWS Certification hub (which AWS refreshed the same day) and the Training and
Certification blog. The good news is the dull news: nothing has moved since the
September announcements. The useful news is that AWS quietly corrected two of
its own pages, and the calendar for the next three months is now consistent
everywhere you look.

## The correction worth knowing about

In September the SAP-C02 and DVA-C02 exam pages said the last day to take each
exam was November **17** and December **1** — one day later than the dates in
AWS's own announcement, which put them at November 16 and November 30 with the
new versions starting the following day. We kept the announcement's dates,
because a table that says "last day X, new version X+1" is internally
consistent and a sentence that disagrees with it is not.

On October 2 AWS edited both exam pages. They now read:

> The last day to take the current exam (SAP-C02) is November 16, 2026.

> The last day to take the current exam (DVA-C02) is November 30, 2026.

If you booked against the later date, check your appointment. If your exam is
on November 17 it is SAP-C03, whatever the booking confirmation was titled when
you made it.

## Two version bumps: what to do

**SAP-C02 → SAP-C03 and DVA-C02 → DVA-C03.** AWS has published neither exam
guide yet; the page for each says the full guide "will be available when
registration opens on October 27, 2026." Until then the only thing you can know
about the new versions is the date.

The practical rule for a version bump on AWS: if you can sit the current version
before its last day, do, because your preparation matches the guide you studied.
If you cannot, wait for October 27, read the new guide's domain weights, and
re-plan rather than assume. The certification you earn is the same either way;
the version number is on the exam, not the badge.

## Two betas, open now

**MLA-C02** (Machine Learning Engineer – Associate) has been in beta since
September 29: 170 minutes, 85 questions, $75 at beta pricing, English only. The
retired MLA-C01 remains available in Korean, Japanese and Simplified Chinese
"until general availability of MLA-C02", so if you read this site in one of
those languages the old exam is still a choice until January.

**AIB-C01** (AI Business Strategist) is AWS's first business-category
certification and is in beta with no published end date: 85 questions, 170
minutes, $50 beta pricing against $100 standard, English and Japanese. Pass
by February 15, 2027 and AWS adds an Early Adopter badge. Beta scores arrive
after the beta window closes, not on the day — the usual trade for the
discount.

## The specialty that ends

Advanced Networking – Specialty (ANS-C01) retires on December 31, 2026 with no
successor exam. AWS's page: "Certifications earned prior to the retirement of
the exam will remain active for the standard three-year period. New AWS
Certified Advanced Networking - Specialty certifications will not be issued
after the retirement of the exam." If it is on your list, the list has a
deadline. Note that Machine Learning – Specialty (MLS-C01) already went the
same way on March 31, 2026, and the MLA page's "next steps" box still
recommends it — stale copy, not a live exam.

## One source that is not a source

AWS maintains a "coming soon" page for certification changes. On October 5 its
only content was the 2025 SysOps-to-CloudOps change; it says nothing about
MLA-C02, SAP-C03, DVA-C03, AIB-C01 or ANS-C01. Treat the per-exam pages and the
monthly Training and Certification blog as the record and ignore that page.

## How the catalogue on this site handles all of this

Each AWS exam card derives its label from its dates at render time, so SAP-C02
reads "Expiring · last day Nov 16, 2026" today and "Retired" on November 17
without an edit, and SAP-C03 reads "Coming · available Nov 17, 2026" and flips
to Active the same morning. The line under the page heading says when the
catalogue was last checked against AWS — October 5, 2026. The next look owed by
hand is October 27, when the new exam guides appear and the two new rows get
their study-guide links.
