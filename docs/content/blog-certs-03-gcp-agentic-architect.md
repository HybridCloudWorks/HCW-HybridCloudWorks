Google publishes fifteen cloud certifications. All fifteen pages, plus the
certification index, were re-read on October 5, 2026. Fourteen are unchanged
in status and title. The fifteenth, the Professional Agentic Architect, has
finished its beta and now has a date — and reading that date correctly is the
whole point of this post.

## The fifteen

| Tier | Credentials |
| --- | --- |
| Foundational | Cloud Digital Leader · Generative AI Leader |
| Associate | Cloud Engineer · Google Workspace Administrator · Data Practitioner |
| Professional | Cloud Architect · Cloud Developer · Data Engineer · Cloud Database Engineer · Cloud DevOps Engineer · Cloud Security Engineer · Cloud Network Engineer · Machine Learning Engineer · Security Operations Engineer · **Agentic Architect** |

No retirement, no rename, no new credential since September. Google's own
"beta certification exams" help page names no running beta. Prices hold:
$99 for the Foundational pair, $125 for Associate, $200 for Professional.

## Agentic Architect: what Google says, exactly

The beta ran from September 8 to September 30, 2026: three hours, around
eighty questions, $120 at a 40% discount off the $200 retail price, English,
and — unusually for a Professional credential — a one-year validity rather than
two. On October 5 the certification page and the index both carry this banner:

> Professional Agentic Architect beta registration is closed. GA registration
> opens November 2.

The beta FAQ fills in the rest:

> The exam GA will be available to the public in mid-November.

> Unlike the beta, the GA exam will follow the standard Google Cloud
> professional exam format: it will be a 2-hour session with official results
> provided in 7–10 business days. If you pass, you will be notified via CM
> Connect and have 2 months to complete the required labs.

> The Professional Agentic Architect certification will cost $200 USD,
> matching standard Google Cloud professional-level exam pricing.

Beta results arrive "in CM Connect at the end of October", and beta passers must
complete the required labs by December 31, 2026.

### Read the date for what it is

November 2 is the day you can **register**. It is not the day you can **sit**
the exam; Google places that at "mid-November" and has not named a day. On this
site's catalogue the credential therefore stays marked Coming with no date,
rather than flipping to Active on November 2 — a status that said "available"
on a day nobody could take the exam would be wrong in the way that costs people
a booking. When Google names the exam day, the row gets it and the label turns
over by itself that morning.

### If you took the beta

Your result lands at the end of October. A pass is a pass — the FAQ is explicit
that "you can pass the beta or GA certification" — but the labs are mandatory
and the deadline is December 31. Put it in the calendar now; a beta pass with
the labs undone is not a certification.

### If you did not

Register on November 2, sit in mid-November, and prepare for a two-hour exam,
not a three-hour one. The exam guide is the beta guide for now; re-read it
when registration opens in case Google revises the domains for GA.

## The rename inside the Machine Learning Engineer exam

The Professional Machine Learning Engineer page carries a note that many
candidates will miss and should not:

> This exam was updated to reflect the transition from Vertex AI to Gemini
> Enterprise Agent Platform, updates to Google Cloud's data and analytics
> stack, and prioritizes Google Cloud native solutions. Please refer to the new
> exam guide for products covered in the new exam.

The exam guide link on the page now ends in `_english_new.pdf`. If your study
material names Vertex AI throughout, the knowledge is largely the same but the
product names on the exam are not, and the data and analytics coverage has
been refreshed. Read the new guide before you book. The index carries the same
caution for every exam — "Our exams are being updated to reflect product
updates announced at Google Cloud Next '26" — and the Cloud Digital Leader,
Associate Cloud Engineer, Cloud Architect, Cloud Developer and Generative AI
Leader pages each say their exam "was updated to reflect recent branding
changes"; Data Practitioner, Data Engineer and Cloud Database Engineer say
theirs "will soon be".

## Renewal without an exam

Four credentials — Cloud Digital Leader, Associate Cloud Engineer, Professional
Cloud Architect and Professional Data Engineer — now show a "Renewal via Google
Skills" section on their pages: a continuing-education path to renew without
re-sitting the exam. It does not change any credential's status, but if yours
is in that set and expires within the year, it is the first option to read.

## How the catalogue on this site handles this

Google does not publish exam codes, so the short forms on the Google Cloud
learning page (PCA, PMLE, PAA and so on) are the community's, not Google's.
Every card's status is derived from its dates at render time; the line under
the heading says when the whole catalogue was last checked — October 5, 2026.
The Agentic Architect card will keep saying Coming until Google names the exam
day, and will say when it does.
